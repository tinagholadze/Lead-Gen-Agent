# Follow-up email template

Use this for the first email to a lead with status **Met**.
Only the parts in `{curly braces}` change. Everything else stays exactly as written.

---

**Subject:** Great meeting you at {where_we_met}, {first_name}

Hi {first_name},

Thanks for the conversation at {where_we_met}.

{personal_lines}

At {{YOUR_BUSINESS}} we help operations teams like yours at {company} take repetitive work off their plate with AI agents: things like status emails, scheduling, invoice matching and weekly reporting.

Would a 20-minute call next week be useful? I can show one or two examples that fit what you described.

Best,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{YOUR_BUSINESS}}

---
{{YOUR_BUSINESS}} · {{YOUR_POSTAL_ADDRESS}}
You're receiving this because we met at {where_we_met}. If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "unsubscribe" and I won't email you again.

---

## Rules for `{personal_lines}`

- 1–2 sentences, written from the Notion fields **Where we met** and **Notes**.
- Refer to something specific they said (e.g. "You mentioned your team still sends shipment updates by hand.").
- If Notes is empty, use one neutral line: "It was good to hear how your team runs operations at {company}."
- Never invent facts, numbers, or names.
