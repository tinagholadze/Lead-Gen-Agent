# US email rules (CAN-SPAM + house rules)

Every email the agents send or draft must pass all checks below.
If any check fails, do not send: skip the lead and log the reason.

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.

## Required by US law (CAN-SPAM Act)

1. **Honest sender.** The From name and address identify the real sender ({{YOUR_NAME}}, {{YOUR_BUSINESS}}).
2. **Honest subject line.** The subject matches the content. No "Re:" or "Fwd:" on a first email, no fake urgency.
3. **Postal address.** Every email includes the business postal address (in the footer).
4. **Clear way to opt out.** Every email says how to stop receiving emails (reply "unsubscribe").
5. **Honor opt-outs fast.** Anyone who opts out is marked Unsubscribed at the next run (the law allows 10 business days; we do it within the hour) and is never emailed again.
6. **Responsibility.** We are responsible for what the agents send on our behalf, so the agent follows these rules even when a lead's data looks unusual.

## House rules (stricter than the law)

7. **Only people we met.** First emails go only to leads with status Met and a filled "Where we met" field.
8. **One follow-up, then wait.** No automatic second or third email. Further emails only as approved replies.
9. **No purchased or guessed addresses.** Use only emails revealed by Apollo or given to us directly.
10. **Replies need human approval.** The agent drafts; a person sends.
11. **US only.** If a lead's country is not US, do not send. Log "non-US lead: needs country rules".

## Before sending: checklist

- [ ] Status is Met and Where we met is filled
- [ ] Not Unsubscribed or Not interested
- [ ] Subject is honest
- [ ] Footer has name, business, postal address and the unsubscribe line
- [ ] No invented facts
